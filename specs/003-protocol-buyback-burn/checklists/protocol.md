# Protocol Requirements Checklist: Buyback, Accrual and Refunds

**Purpose**: Review the completeness, clarity, consistency and measurability of continuous protocol-fee accrual, unused-time refund backing, Safe administration and Pons buyback requirements before task breakdown and implementation.
**Created**: 2026-09-07
**Feature**: [Specification](../spec.md) · [Implementation plan](../plan.md)
**Depth**: Standard, prioritized by accounting and custody risk.
**Audience / Timing**: Requirements reviewer; after the accepted accrual extension and before implementation task approval.

**Note**: This custom checklist was generated by `$speckit-checklist` from the accepted feature scope. It reviews written requirements, not implementation behavior or passing software tests.
**Review Ownership**: This is a reviewer-owned requirements-quality artifact. Mark an item `[x]` only when the reviewer determines its criterion is satisfied.
**Marker Semantics**: `[x]` means requirements quality was reviewed and satisfied; it does not mean implementation is complete. All newly generated items are unchecked.

## Requirement Completeness

- [ ] CHK001 Are the default, minimum, maximum, precision, combined-allocation limit and publication-time permanence of the protocol allocation fully specified? [Completeness, Spec §FR-003–FR-005]
- [ ] CHK002 Are allocated fees, protected unearned reserves, earned fees awaiting collection, released inventory, refunded contributions and completed burns each defined with a custody boundary? [Completeness, Spec §FR-008, §FR-018, §FR-028, §FR-038–FR-040]
- [ ] CHK003 Are refund authority, recipient, unchanged gross entitlement, reserve-first funding order and owner top-up ceilings specified for creator shutdown as well as ordinary cancellation? [Completeness, Spec §FR-006, §FR-039; Accrual §Refund sequence]
- [ ] CHK004 Is the prohibition on refunding already earned protocol fees explicit both before and after collection, while preserving creator, reward and referral allocation timing? [Completeness, Spec §FR-006, §Assumptions; Accrual §Refund sequence]
- [ ] CHK005 Are fee earning rules specified for queued paid duration, complimentary grants and zero-gross paid periods, including their effect on later purchased time? [Completeness, Spec §FR-038; Accrual §Per-member paid clock and lots]
- [ ] CHK006 Is the authority boundary complete for creators, supporters, ordinary collectors, the protocol Safe, the developer beneficiary and external launchpad administrators? [Completeness, Spec §FR-001, §FR-009–FR-012, §FR-023, §FR-026, §FR-037, §FR-040]
- [ ] CHK007 Are the exclusions of DAO governance, a custom administration screen, discretionary earned-fee payouts and compatibility with obsolete fee paths explicit across the scope documents? [Completeness, Spec §Scope replacement, §FR-010–FR-011, §FR-037; Plan §Summary]

## Requirement Clarity

- [ ] CHK008 Is continuous earning defined against consumed paid time, with an unambiguous start, endpoint and cancellation boundary independent of calendar periods and collection timing? [Clarity, Spec §FR-038–FR-040; Accrual §Per-member paid clock and lots]
- [ ] CHK009 Are payment rounding, cumulative earned rounding and cancellation rounding separately defined, including the stated one-raw-unit cancellation residual bound? [Clarity, Spec §FR-004, §FR-038–FR-039; Accrual §Refund sequence]
- [ ] CHK010 Are forecast windows, original payment units, timestamp, coverage and refund assumptions defined so projected fee releases cannot be confused with guaranteed buybacks or acquired-token amounts? [Clarity, Spec §FR-028, §SC-012; Accrual §Views, forecasts and evidence]
- [ ] CHK011 Is the Safe's responsibility for acceptable economic prices distinguished from objective fair-value assurance, including the limits of protection against a mistaken or compromised Safe? [Clarity, Spec §FR-015, §FR-026, §FR-037; Policy §Economic authority, §Choosing reference evidence]
- [ ] CHK012 Are initial policy defaults distinguished from hard bounds and later Safe-adjustable values for prices, validity, spending exposure and processing amounts? [Clarity, Spec §FR-015, §FR-036; Policy §Initial settings and hard bounds]
- [ ] CHK013 Is “eligible for buyback” defined distinctly from earned entitlement and collection readiness, with price, policy, liquidity, gas and launch-state prerequisites stated? [Clarity, Spec §FR-012–FR-016, §FR-040; Policy §Policy changes and failure states]

## Requirement Consistency

- [ ] CHK014 Do the spec, plan, data model, interface descriptions and acceptance evidence consistently require earned-only release, with all superseded upfront-release statements identified or removed? [Consistency, Spec §Clarifications, §FR-008, §FR-038–FR-040; Plan §Architecture and Implementation Strategy; Data Model §Fee accrual and refund backing]
- [ ] CHK015 Is full unused-time refund backing at a 100% allocation consistent across creator-facing requirements, refund funding rules, rounding definitions and success criteria? [Consistency, Spec §FR-005, §FR-039, §SC-011; Accrual §Refund sequence]
- [ ] CHK016 Are payment allocations, tier releases, vault receipts, donations, refunds and venue costs distinguished consistently so accounting requirements cannot count a transfer or cost twice? [Consistency, Spec §FR-004, §FR-007, §FR-018, §SC-002; Data Model §Fee inventory; Accrual §Views, forecasts and evidence]
- [ ] CHK017 Are residual converted assets required to retain their original fee/donation attribution and burn purpose, even when their denomination differs from the original membership payment? [Consistency, Spec §FR-017–FR-018; Policy §Settlement sequence]
- [ ] CHK018 Are automatic normal graduation, public recovery and exceptional Safe-dependent route repairs described consistently without implying either universal operator independence or mandatory routine Safe intervention? [Consistency, Spec §User Story 5, §FR-025; Plan §External Feasibility Gates]

## Acceptance Criteria Quality

- [ ] CHK019 Do the 12-period example, fractional-period earning and 100% refund-backing criteria specify exact amounts, timing assumptions and funding components? [Measurability, Spec §User Story 2, §SC-011; Accrual §Refund sequence]
- [ ] CHK020 Are conservation and refund/collection ordering criteria explicit about which entitlements, refund amounts and aggregate balances must agree, rather than requiring incidental transaction histories to be identical? [Measurability, Spec §SC-002, §SC-012; Accrual §Views, forecasts and evidence]
- [ ] CHK021 Do buyback success requirements distinguish positive acquired output, actual input consumption, unused funds and attributable supply destruction from mere transfers, locks or vesting? [Measurability, Spec §FR-014, §FR-017, §SC-003; Policy §Settlement sequence]
- [ ] CHK022 Are the required evidence classes and completion criteria precise enough to prevent source review, sampled arithmetic, simulated authority or mocked markets from being substituted for authentic Safe, fork and browser evidence? [Acceptance Criteria, Spec §FR-031–FR-035, §SC-008; Plan §External Feasibility Gates]

## Scenario Coverage

- [ ] CHK023 Are accrual and refund requirements complete for single-period purchases, multi-period prepayment, renewals, gifts and variable contributions with different amounts? [Coverage, Spec §FR-006, §FR-038, §SC-011; Accrual §Per-member paid clock and lots]
- [ ] CHK024 Are requirements defined for expiry, credential removal, ownership changes, cancellation and rejoining without losing earned entitlements or reviving canceled fee schedules? [Coverage, Spec §FR-038–FR-040, §SC-011; Accrual §Generation reset and bounded work]
- [ ] CHK025 Are stopped collection, replacement collectors, repeated collection and refund/collection races covered without making fee entitlement depend on automation availability? [Coverage, Spec §FR-040, §SC-005, §SC-012]
- [ ] CHK026 Are token disablement, global/per-asset buyback pauses and route/policy repairs specified independently from membership access, continuous earning, collection and refunds? [Coverage, Spec §FR-002, §FR-008, §FR-016, §FR-036, §FR-040]
- [ ] CHK027 Are requirements defined for failed asset transfers, frozen tokens, unavailable markets and partial data availability, with the affected scope and recovery limits stated? [Coverage, Spec §FR-007–FR-008, §FR-019, §FR-029; Interfaces §Public product contract]
