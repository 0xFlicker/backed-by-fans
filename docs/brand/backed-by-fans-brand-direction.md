# Backed By Fans Brand Direction

## Brand Foundation

| Element | Working direction |
|---|---|
| Public name | **Backed By Fans** |
| Product descriptor | Creator-owned memberships |
| Positioning | A creator-owned membership rail for creators and the people who make their work possible. |
| Promise | Creators control the membership; supporters can keep a durable membership record. |
| Primary tagline | **Creator-owned. Backed By Fans.** |
| Campaign line | Your work. Your membership. Your people. |
| Supporter line | Show up. Stay part of the story. |

Use **Backed By Fans** with spaces and title capitalization on every public surface. Use `backed-by-fans` for repository, package, domain-path, and deployment identifiers. Use `BackedByFans` only where a code identifier cannot contain spaces or hyphens. Do not use `BackedByFans` as the public wordmark.

## Message Hierarchy

The homepage should lead with the creator-fan relationship rather than blockchain infrastructure.

> Creator-owned memberships
>
> Your people make your work possible.
>
> Create a membership your fans can join directly, with terms you control and membership records they keep.

Primary creator action: **Create a membership**
Primary supporter action: **Explore memberships**

Explain direct ownership, immutable economics, rewards, USDG, and Robinhood Chain progressively. Wallet and chain language belongs near the action that requires it, not in the opening brand promise.

Backed By Fans provides membership terms, payment, renewal, rewards, and onchain credentials. Creators remain responsible for the content, access, and community experiences attached to their tiers. A gifted credential records membership, not proof that its holder personally paid or participated.

## Voice

- **Warm, not sentimental:** celebrate the relationship without treating fans as an audience metric.
- **Direct, not technical:** prefer “join,” “renew,” “support,” and “claim” to protocol jargon.
- **Confident, not grandiose:** explain durable ownership and fixed rules without “revolutionary” or “the future of” claims.
- **Transparent, not alarming:** show permanent choices, payment splits, and transaction states in plain language.
- **Human, not institutional:** creators and supporters are “you,” not accounts, users, or liquidity.

### Product language

| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Create a membership | Launch a financial product |
| Join or renew | Invest or stake |
| Support a creator | Buy exposure to a creator |
| Membership rewards | Yield, dividends, or passive income |
| Reward share | Equity, ownership in a creator, or profit share |
| Fixed membership terms | Guaranteed returns |
| Directly onchain | Trustless, permissionless, or decentralized as unexplained slogans |

“Backed” describes support for a creator. It must never imply equity in a creator, a security, or a promise that the membership will appreciate.

## Visual Direction

The visual system should feel like a live room before a show: warm light, dark structure, individual expression, and a visible crowd. It should not look like an exchange, a DeFi dashboard, or a generic neon crypto brand.

### Palette

| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Backstage | `#11131A` | Primary text, dark fields, navigation |
| House Light | `#F7F2E8` | Primary canvas and warm neutral |
| Applause | `#FF6A4D` | Main action and emotional accent |
| Fan Blue | `#625BFF` | Links, verified states, technical accent |
| Encore | `#D9F99D` | Success, active membership, highlight |
| Quiet | `#707386` | Secondary decoration and large-text support only |

Use Backstage on House Light, Backstage on Applause, white on Fan Blue, and Backstage on Encore for accessible text. Accent colors should not carry status without text or an icon. Quiet is not suitable for normal-size body text on Backstage.

### Typography

- **Display:** Instrument Serif for creator names, editorial headlines, and moments of personality.
- **Interface:** Geist Sans for navigation, forms, transaction states, and dense product information.
- **Technical:** Geist Mono for addresses, transaction hashes, amounts when tabular alignment matters, and contract facts.

The contrast between an expressive display face and a restrained interface face keeps the brand creator-led without weakening trust in financial interactions.

### Graphic language

- Use layered, slightly offset shapes to show a creator visibly supported by many people.
- Let creator photography, artwork, avatars, and tier imagery provide most of the variation.
- Use subtle fan-shaped arcs or repeated cards as framing devices, never as decorative clutter.
- Prefer confident flat color and tactile grain over glassmorphism, token coins, network-node diagrams, and luminous gradients.
- Motion may open or settle stacked layers, but every state must remain clear with reduced motion enabled.

## Mark Direction

The recommended concept is **The Backing Stack**: two or three offset rounded forms sit behind one clear foreground form. It expresses many fans supporting one creator, maps naturally to membership cards, and stays legible at favicon size without using a coin, chain link, or wallet symbol.

The primary logo should be a custom **Backed By Fans** wordmark in title case. The stack mark can stand alone for app icons and social avatars. `BBF` is acceptable as an internal shorthand but should not lead the public identity until the full name is established.

## Initial Asset Set

- Primary horizontal wordmark and one-color variants
- Backing Stack standalone mark
- App icon and favicon set
- Social avatar and Open Graph template
- Light and dark presentation examples
- Core color and typography tokens for the web application
- Creator tier image frame and fallback artwork
- Brand copy examples for homepage, creator setup, supporter join, and transaction recovery

## Naming and Clearance Note

The phrase “backed by fans” is intuitive and is already used descriptively in adjacent creator and fan-funding language. That makes the name easy to understand, but the working direction still needs professional trademark, domain, and social-handle clearance before a public launch. A web search is not legal clearance.
