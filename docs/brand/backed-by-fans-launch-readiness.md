# Backed By Fans Brand Launch Readiness

This is an evidence checklist, not a declaration that the brand is cleared. Leave an item open until its owner and supporting evidence are recorded.

## Naming

- [ ] Professional trademark review covers the intended name, jurisdictions, and relevant product categories.
- [ ] The owning entity and filing strategy are recorded.
- [ ] Primary production domain and defensive domains are acquired.
- [ ] Required social and developer handles are acquired or dispositioned.
- [ ] The exact public capitalization **Backed By Fans** is approved for launch.

## Identity and Assets

- [ ] `docs/brand/backed-by-fans-brand-direction.md` is promoted from `working` to an approved launch status.
- [ ] Primary wordmark, one-color variants, and standalone mark have reviewed source files and exports.
- [ ] App icons, favicon, Open Graph template, and fallback tier artwork are approved at required sizes.
- [ ] Font sources, licenses, fallback stacks, and permitted deployment uses are recorded.
- [ ] Every first-party brand asset has a recorded source, owner, license, and permitted use.
- [ ] Starter Next.js and Vercel identity has been removed from public surfaces.

## Product Copy

- [ ] The product is described as a membership rail rather than a hosted content or community platform.
- [ ] Gifted memberships are not presented as proof that the recipient personally supported the creator.
- [ ] Permanent reward shares, dilution, and the absence of guaranteed return are explained before deployment and payment.
- [ ] Refund limitations and fixed payout destinations are explained before payment.
- [ ] No public copy promises creator equity, investment exposure, yield, dividends, passive income, or guaranteed returns.

## Release Evidence

- [ ] Public pages, titles, metadata, and social-preview text use **Backed By Fans** exactly.
- [ ] Package, repository, route, and deployment identifiers use `backed-by-fans` where a spaced name is impossible.
- [ ] The standalone mark, not an illegible full wordmark, is used for favicon and app-icon contexts.
- [ ] Approved text and background pairs meet WCAG AA contrast requirements.
- [ ] Final launch review records the owner, evidence link, and disposition for every item above.
